One <canvas>, one WebGL2 context, two Terminals attached to one TerminalSurface. The chequered areas are parts of the shared drawing buffer that no grid was placed over.

Hide pane B sets its overlay to display: none — the path a tabbed host takes. Its area goes back to chequerboard because nothing is placed there any more, while its grid, its cells and its font configuration’s atlas all stay resident: showing it again is a placement, not a rebuild, and surface.bakes() does not move across the trip. Its content keeps advancing while hidden — a terminal that is set aside is not a terminal that is stopped.
